# E-commerce Search Engine Project

## Project overview

You are hired as a short-term consulting team by an online retail company to design, implement, and evaluate improvements to the site's search engine.
The company seeks measurable gains in search result quality, although not assessed in this project, latency and operational costs can also be considered.

Your objectives are to gather requirements, propose a plan and build search engines, evaluate them against held-out queries, and present actionable recommendations. The project is structured as a series of assignments that build on each other, finishing with a final presentation to the client.

## Use case

An e-commerce website has a catalog of products and has observed that customers frequently fail to find relevant products, leading to lost conversions. It remains unclear what could cause this problem. The website owners provide a set of product data and a set of queries for evaluation and want your team to investigate the problem and propose solutions.

Your team is tasked with investigating common reasons for poor search performance in the domain of product search, and to propose and implement improvements to the search engine. You will evaluate your proposed solutions using a set of held-out queries and relevance judgments, and present your findings to the client.

## Objectives

  * Investigate common reasons for poor search performance in the domain of product search
  * Propose and implement improvements to the search engine
  * Evaluate your proposed solutions for a set of held-out queries using relevance judgments and log data
  * Present your findings to the client in a clear and actionable manner

## Deliverables

  * Documentation of your investigation, proposed solutions, and evaluation results (i.e., lab book and final presentation slides)
  * Run files for the different search engine configurations you implemented
  * Relevance judgments and log data for the held-out queries
